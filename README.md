# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze pizza sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, and revenue trends using transactional sales data.

The dashboard transforms raw sales records into actionable business insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify top-performing products, understand customer preferences, and make data-driven decisions to improve profitability and operational efficiency.

### Key Highlights

* Analyzed 21K+ customer orders and 50K+ pizzas sold.
* Generated insights from $817K+ in total revenue.
* Built interactive dashboards using Power BI, DAX, and Power Query.
* Identified best-selling and underperforming products.
* Analyzed sales trends by category, size, month, and day.
* Developed KPI-driven reports for business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Pizza businesses generate thousands of sales transactions daily. Without proper analytics, it becomes difficult to identify customer preferences, sales trends, and revenue opportunities.

This dashboard was built to answer critical business questions:

* Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
* Which products drive the highest order volume?
* What pizza sizes are most preferred by customers?
* Which days and months produce peak sales?
* Which products require optimization or promotion?
* How can sales and inventory planning be improved?

The goal is to provide a centralized analytics solution that supports strategic business decisions and revenue growth.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Customer Purchasing Behavior

* Highest order volume occurs during weekends.
* Friday and Saturday evenings are peak sales periods.

### Product Performance

* Classic pizzas are the most popular category.
* Large-size pizzas generate the highest revenue contribution.

### Revenue Analysis

* Total revenue exceeded $817K.
* Average customer spending is approximately $38 per order.

### Top Performers

* Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue.
* Classic Deluxe Pizza recorded the highest sales volume and order count.

### Optimization Opportunities

* Brie Carre Pizza consistently ranked lowest across revenue, quantity sold, and orders.
* Product optimization and targeted marketing could improve performance.
